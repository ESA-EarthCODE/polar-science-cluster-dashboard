Antarctica InSync is a global effort to synchronise research across Antarctica and the Southern Ocean, connecting ice, ocean, climate, and life to protect this vital region.

As part of the initiative, and to facilitate easier analysis of and access to scientific Antarctica data, several datasets are combined into zarr stores, reprojected to a common 100x100m grid in the EPSG:3031 Antarctic Polar Stereographic projection.

See [Antarctica Insync website](https://www.antarctica-insync.org/) for more information on the activities.
